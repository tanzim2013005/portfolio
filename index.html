Core Expertise

My Research Background

Focused investigation spanning cobalt-free battery cathodes, multifunctional polymer nanocomposites, and rigorous materials diagnostics.

01 Energy Storage & Batteries

Advanced Battery Materials & Cell Architecture

Examined relationships among dopant concentration, crystal structure, phase evolution, charge-transfer behavior, and electrochemical performance in sol–gel-derived, cobalt-free LiNiO₂ cathodes.

  • • Cathode & Lattice Modification: Engineered synergistic Mg²⁺/Al³⁺ co-doping to suppress Li⁺/Ni²⁺ cation mixing, relieve lattice microstrain, and mitigate H2→H3 phase distortion.
  • • Electrode & Device Fabrication: Synthesized and coated both cathode and graphite-anode slurries, fabricating and evaluating both coin and pouch cells for rechargeable energy storage.
  • • Interfacial Charge Kinetics: Analyzed multi-cycle cyclic polarization and EIS equivalent-circuit modeling (Rs, Rct, CPE, W; ZSimpWin) to deconvolute charge transfer and Li⁺ diffusion.
LiNiO₂ Cathodes Coin & Pouch Cells EIS & CV Kinetics
02 Smart Materials & Polymers

Multifunctional Polymer Nanocomposites

Synthesized electroactive oxide nanoparticles (BaTiO₃, BaFe₁₂O₁₉, and Bi₄Ti₃O₁₂) and reinforced them into flexible PMMA and PVDF polymer matrices via controlled solution casting.

  • • Nanoparticle Synthesis: Solid-state calcination (800 °C) and sol-gel complexation routes.
  • • Dielectric & Piezo Response: Evaluated MWS interfacial polarization (high ε' ≈ 10.26, tanδ = 0.08) and piezoelectric d₃₃ up to 40 pC/N.
  • • Environmental Remediation: Achieved 67.2% photocatalytic degradation of Methylene Blue under UV-Vis irradiation.
BFO & BTO / PMMA Piezoelectric d₃₃ Dielectric & Photocatalysis
03 Materials Diagnostics

Comprehensive Materials Diagnostics

Applied multi-technique characterization to resolve crystal structure, nanoscale surface morphology, electronic transitions, and pyrolytic decomposition kinetics.

  • • Structural & Morphology: XRD Williamson–Hall microstrain/crystallite sizing; FE-SEM (100,000×) + ImageJ particle size distributions; EDS mapping.
  • • Bonding & Optics: ATR-FTIR metal–oxygen vibrational modes (Ni–O, Fe–O, Ba–O); UV–Vis DRS & Tauc optical bandgap determination.
  • • Thermal Analysis: Simultaneous TGA/DTA/DTG pyrolytic mass-loss and phase evolution analysis up to 800 °C.
Williamson–Hall XRD FE-SEM / EDS FTIR / UV-Vis / TGA

Current Progress & Trajectory

Ongoing Research & Manuscripts in Active Preparation

I am currently expanding the analytical modeling, electrochemical endurance verification, and device-level prototyping for several mature projects nearing submission:

Structural Evolution and Electrochemical Performance of Sol-Gel-Derived Mg/Al Co-Doped LiNiO₂ Cathodes for Lithium-Ion Batteries

Current Focus: Finalizing prolonged capacity retention cycler data at 0.5C/1C rates, deconvoluting dynamic phase transformation kinetics from differential capacity curves (dQ/dV), and correlating microstrain reduction with long-term lattice integrity.

Interfacial Polarization and Energy Storage Performance of (Ba, Cd) Co-doped Bi₄Ti₃O₁₂ Nanoparticle Reinforced PVDF Nanocomposites

Current Focus: Evaluating interfacial Maxwell-Wagner-Sillars (MWS) dielectric relaxation, electroactive β-phase nucleation in PVDF polymer chains, and high-field energy storage density calculation from P-E hysteresis loops.

🔬 Continuing Device Prototyping & Laboratory Scaling:

In RUET's Energy Materials Lab, I am actively extending coin-cell formulation procedures toward multi-layer pouch-cell configurations. This includes investigating drying gradient effects on thick electrode cracking, optimizing binder (PVDF vs. aqueous binders) interactions, and establishing elevated-temperature (45 °C–55 °C) electrochemical impedance protocols.
